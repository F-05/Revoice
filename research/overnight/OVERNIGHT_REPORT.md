## 15. Remaining-error attribution
- Candidate coverage: no longer the binding constraint (oracle 0.153).
- CANDIDATE DISCRIMINATION is the bottleneck: rankers get WORSE as the pool
  grows (frozen 0.2760 vs P3 0.2922) — a 16-feature scorer cannot pick safely
  among 15+ near-duplicates. Missed switches: capture is 39% of even the small
  pool's headroom.
- Cheap independent signals are weak-to-pathological standalone (w2v/gpt2
  argmin ≈ 0.94–1.53 WER; medium.en mean-NLL confounded; only sum-NLL usable).
- Synthetic v1 taught the wrong prior (needs a real-A0-anchored recipe:
  corrupt AROUND actual production transcripts, keep is_a0 semantics).
- Data scarcity blocks the biggest lever (adaptation) locally.

## 16. Was ASR fine-tuning promising?
Scientifically YES (literature: ~10–16% WER with hours-scale adaptation);
practically BLOCKED tonight: whisper-small seq2seq training on this MPS stack
ran ~75 s/step (~4 h/epoch) across two attempts (first also starved by decode
contention). Unadapted small.en val WER 0.951 shows the headroom. This is the
top next-step, on a cloud GPU.

## 17. Best combination
Frozen H1-H5 pool + 16-feature listwise ranker whose decisive feature is
teacher-forced medium.en sum-NLL (+ consensus/medoid + gpt2/w2v z-scores).
Expanded pools help only the oracle, not (yet) the system.
